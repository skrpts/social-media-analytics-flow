## Capability

Builds a hashtag and topic strategy grounded in how existing hashtags actually perform, the audience profile, and the content pillars the performance model identifies.

## When to Use

- Hashtag reach has plateaued or declined
- Entering a new niche where the tag landscape is unfamiliar
- Aligning tags to content pillars rather than picking them per post

## Inputs

Audience insights, content performance model, performance report, platforms, industry

## Outputs

Tiered hashtag sets per pillar, topic strategy, and a measurement approach
