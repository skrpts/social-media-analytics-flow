## Capability

Evaluates cross-platform performance against business goals, balancing quantitative results with strategic value, and recommends where time, budget and creative effort should move.

## When to Use

- Managing three or more platforms with finite capacity
- Deciding whether to exit or double down on a channel
- Justifying a reallocation of social media spend

## Inputs

Platform performance data, platforms being compared, account context

## Outputs

Per-platform comparison with ROI assessment and a resource reallocation recommendation
